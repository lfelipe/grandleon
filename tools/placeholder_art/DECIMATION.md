# What the Nintendo 64 does not draw

Generated by `tools/placeholder_art/generate.py`. Do not hand-edit.

The console draws a 32-texel cell into a cell of 14 to 26 pixels and **point samples figures** (`play_rom.cpp`: "the ground is filtered and the figures are not"). A native column the sampler skips is not dimmed, it is dropped. This page is the measurement of that, taken on the same native canvases the build drew; `profiles.LEGIBILITY` measures a different reduction through a box filter and structurally cannot see it.

## The dead margin

Column and row **31** are sampled at no cell size at all, and 3361 of 3361 figure cells draw on them. Every texel there is drawn, stored, uploaded and seen by nobody on this console.
