Production normalization
========================

The built-in output was deterministically resized from 1774x887 to 1772x886 with Lanczos filtering. Each 443px cell then received a 5px safety crop and was resized to 385px inside a centered 443px transparent cell; alpha values below 5% were cleared and fully transparent RGB was zeroed. This guarantees empty cell-edge gutters, clean transparency, and consistent production occupancy. No generative edits were applied after the original prompt.
