# Godot character art

This directory holds the curated high-resolution sprite rosters used by the
recommended PC graphics profile. Both the sci-fi and mythical directions have
separate male and female characters for every archetype. Each character is a
transparent 4×2 sheet with 443×443 cells in row-major order:

1. idle A;
2. idle B;
3. walk A;
4. walk B;
5. weapon wind-up;
6. weapon follow-through;
7. relay cast;
8. hurt or defeat.

The images were generated specifically for this project, then trimmed to exact
cell boundaries. The runtime consumes these committed PNGs directly;
screenshots of them in play are therefore implementation evidence rather than
concept art.

Each mythical and sci-fi sheet has an adjacent `*_team_mask.png`. Godot uses
that mask to render blue, red, green, violet, amber, or bone clothing and armour
while leaving faces, hair, equipment, natural features, and effects untouched.
A missing or malformed mask keeps the source colour. A missing sheet or invalid
character binding restores only that character's generated 32-pixel fallback.
Neither fallback changes portable game content, packages, or rules.

Bindings, masks, pivots, and timing live in
`content/graphics_profiles.json`. The human review images and regeneration
commands are in the [artwork guide](../../../../../docs/ARTWORK.md).

`relay_commander.glb` is an imported, skinned 3D character. It contains a
sixteen-bone humanoid skeleton, seven material surfaces, and six in-place clips
exported with deliberately non-semantic DCC names. The full-voxel profile maps
those names explicitly, proving that a replacement art pack does not need to
adopt the engine's spelling. Its ceramic armour, helmet and visor, relay core,
shield, sword, and cape are readable at the real board scale.

The GLB is generated by `platform/godot/tools/export_relay_commander.py` through
Blender. `relay_rogue.glb` and `relay_stormcaller.glb` use the exact same
sixteen-bone hierarchy with distinct silhouettes, equipment, materials, and
action sets. `relay_drone.glb` uses the same pipeline with its own fifteen-bone
quadruped skeleton, articulated legs and jaw, twin rotors, relay core, seven
materials, and six explicitly mapped actions. Run
`platform/godot/scripts/generate-imported-characters.sh` to reproduce all four.
The checked-in binaries are the player inputs; Blender is needed only to revise
the source assets and is not shipped in a PC bundle.

`relay_commander_3d.tscn` and `relay_drone_3d.tscn` are procedural references.
The Full Voxel profile binds imported GLBs for the four
Last Signal encounter identities: commander, rogue, stormcaller, and beast.
Knight, archer, mage, and healer keep the procedural voxel fallback until they
receive authored GLBs. This is separate from the complete eight-archetype sprite
roster. See the [artwork guide](../../../../../docs/ARTWORK.md) for the
human-facing coverage table and previews.
