# Emberbound: The Last Dawn

This is the single player-facing Grand Leon campaign used by the PC build. It
contains five escalating battles, including the opening movement-and-attack
tutorial and the final Regent Veyr boss fight.

Auren, Rowanfield's bell-ringer, and Senna, an apprentice mapmaker, awaken a
pair of embers when each turns back to save the other. They are chosen by that
decision rather than a bloodline: their task is to reach the First Flame before
Regent Veyr freezes a failing world in an unchanging present. Brann and Ilyra
join after the tutorial; Oren and Tess join after the second battle. Across the
causeway and the vault, Auren grows from an anxious would-be commander into a
leader who trusts the company, while Senna stops treating inherited maps as the
limits of what can be changed. The ending resolves that argument by sharing the
flame among communities instead of handing it to another ruler.

Every encounter advances the story only on victory. A defeat follows its own
short ending and returns to a terminal state where the player can load a
checkpoint or begin again; it can never fall through into victory dialogue.
The deterministic advancing-party measurement wins all five solo battles. Its
final boss run reaches round ten with one survivor, providing a deliberately
hard baseline without requiring optimal play merely to prove the campaign is
finishable.

The source is deliberately portable. In a one-player session every allied
placement belongs to player one. In two-player local co-op, Auren, Brann and
Oren form player one's army while Senna, Ilyra and Tess form player two's. The
same ownership records are consumed by PC, Nintendo 64 and PlayStation builds;
only optional local-model behaviour is PC-gated.

The older projects under `games/demo` remain conformance fixtures. They are not
part of the player-facing game list and should not receive new campaign work.
